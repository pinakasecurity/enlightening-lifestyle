About the practice

A quiet room in a loud world.

Enlightening Lifestyle is the practice of Ashna Ddhannak, a holistic life coach working from Mumbai since 2014. It sits at the meeting point of therapy, spiritual practice and the practical craft of arranging a space.

Most people arrive with something specific: a body that needs rest, a decision that keeps circling, a workspace that never shifts the way the day actually runs.

The work is close and specific. Some sessions are received on the body through Reiki, Reiki Angel Therapy, Quantum Healing and Anmol Healing. Some are conversations about a life pattern, a decision, or a family. Some begin with the house or the workplace and practical changes to a space.

Nothing here is packaged as a cure or a promise. Sessions are held individually and by appointment, and the same quiet expectation carries through readings, remedies and workshops.
